Questions

Before you join.

Is this financial advice?

No. Angel shares her personal journey, mindset, and habits. She never recommends specific investments or promises returns. For decisions about your own money, speak to a licensed professional.

Are sessions in English or Chinese?

Both. Angel teaches in English with Mandarin whenever it helps, and all notes come in both languages.

What if I can't make a live call?

Every Circle and Dinner Table session is recorded. Replays are available the same day.

Can I cancel or get a refund?

The Circle can be cancelled anytime. The Dinner Table and One-on-One are refundable in full up to 7 days before your first session.
